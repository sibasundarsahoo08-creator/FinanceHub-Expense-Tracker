# FinanceHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: **[ADD YOUR RELEASE DATE]**

FinanceHub helps users record and understand their personal finances. This policy explains the information handled by the application.

## Information collected

FinanceHub stores account information (username and email), financial records entered by the user (income, expenses, budgets, savings goals and recurring transactions), payment-method labels, notes, and receipt files voluntarily uploaded by the user.

FinanceHub does not request bank credentials, card numbers, UPI PINs, device contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers or health information.

## How information is used

Information is used only for local authentication, financial summaries, reports and exports requested by the user. The offline mobile application does not synchronize records with a server.

## Storage and security

Mobile records are stored in the application's private SQLite database on the user's device. The current local account identifier is kept in device secure storage. Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes and are not stored as plain text. Users should still avoid entering confidential bank credentials in notes or receipt files.

## Sharing

FinanceHub does not sell or automatically transmit mobile financial information. Data leaves the device only when the user explicitly shares an exported PDF, Excel or CSV file.

## Data retention and deletion

Records remain available until the user deletes individual items, deletes the local account from **Settings → Delete Account and Data**, clears application storage, or uninstalls the application. Uninstalling may permanently remove all locally stored records.

## Children

FinanceHub is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law.
